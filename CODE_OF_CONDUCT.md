# Code of conduct

Makhtoot contributors work across languages, writing traditions and levels of experience. Discuss the behavior of a glyph, a design decision or a patch with specific examples. Give people room to ask questions in Arabic or English. Credit the people and foundries whose work you share.

Harassment, threats, slurs, unwanted personal contact, and sharing someone else's private information are unacceptable in the project's issues, pull requests and other project spaces.

Send a private report to the maintainer at **support@binjamshid.com** with “Makhtoot conduct” in the subject. Include the location of the incident, the behavior and the action you need, if known. Share personal information only as needed. The maintainer may ask for clarification, remove content, close a discussion or restrict participation according to the circumstances.
